Pass odds come from resampling each strategy's own trades (bootstrap), so they assume trades are independent and the future resembles the past. Avg time to pass = average trades to pass at this strategy's own historical pace of trading (needs exit times). Exp. time and fees to pass count failed attempts too: each failure restarts, so expected time = average attempt length ÷ pass odds and expected fees = fee ÷ pass odds (an attempt that times out counts its full length). Efficiency = profit ÷ max drawdown, forced to −99 under 100 trades. Not trading advice.
